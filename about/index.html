About Prospry

A quiet life of quality, applied to AI.

Prospry exists to partner with ambitious business owners — simplifying operations, sharpening systems, and building the kind of foundation that grows stronger as the business does, with the care and craft it deserves.

Who's behind it

Jared spent five years living inside a company's Salesforce org — architecture, automation, data model, deployments, all of it. He owns the full sandbox promotion path end to end, with no separate QA function, because he'd rather catch a problem early than clean one up later.

That work taught him a blunt lesson: most businesses don't have a technology problem, they have a clarity problem. More fields, more steps, more tools rarely means more control — usually it means less. The businesses that win are the ones that strip things back, build with intention, and never treat a small detail as too small to matter.

Prospry is that same discipline, applied to AI. Jared started it to be the guide he wishes more business owners had — someone who tells you plainly what's actually worth doing with AI, and then does the work of building it, quietly, in the background.

What we hold to

The standard behind every engagement, whether it's a single call or an ongoing partnership.

Craft

We build things the right way, not the fast way. Good enough isn't good enough — the quality of our work is a statement about how much we respect our clients and ourselves.

Clarity

Speak the truth in love

Sweat the details

Raving fans
